Support

For questions or problems with Alya Steps, Block Nest and Yarn Bloom, write to us: info@taykosan.com

Where is my progress stored? Only on your phone. If you delete the game, your progress is deleted too; it does not move to a new phone.

Do I need the internet? No, the games play offline.

Do I need an account or to pay? No. There are no accounts, ads or in-app purchases.

How do I turn off notifications? In your phone's settings, turn off the game's notification permission.
